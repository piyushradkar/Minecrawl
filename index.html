Welcome to the Daily MineCrawl Challenge!

Every day a new mineral deposit is discovered. All operators are given this deposit to mine. Can you top today's leaderboard?


How to Operate:

  • Click on grid blocks to mine and excavate them.
  • Avoid mining unnecessary waste blocks (blue blocks with negative values).
  • You earn profit when you mine high-grade gold blocks (gold-yellow blocks).
  • Time is money: block values are continuously discounted at 1% per second! Excavate high-grade blocks as early as possible.
  • Note: Clicks for mining blocks are limited to a maximum of 3 clicks per second.

No underground mining allowed.

Open-pit rules apply: To excavate a block, the block directly above it and both diagonal blocks above (left and right) must be completely mined first.
